Dimensions used
  • Playing area: 3,569 × 1,778 mm (11 ft 8½ in × 5 ft 10 in)
  • Balls: 52.5 mm diameter
  • Baulk line: 737 mm from the bottom cushion face
  • D: semicircle of 292 mm radius on the baulk line
  • Brown, yellow, green: centre and ends of the D; yellow on the right from baulk
  • Blue: centre spot. Pink: midway between blue and top cushion
  • Black: 324 mm from the top cushion face
  • Reds: apex as close behind the pink as possible without touching
  • Corner pockets: 89 mm at the fall, jaws cut along the pocket diagonal
  • Middle pockets: 108 mm at the fall, rounded jaws flaring to the nose
  • Cushion rubber: 50 mm from nose to rail

Keyboard: with a ball selected, arrow keys nudge it 1 mm (Shift for 10 mm), Delete takes it off the table, Ctrl+Z undoes.

Off the table

Tap a ball to respot it, or drag it onto the table.
